How to use
  1. Set your monthly cap. Enter your plan's maximum credits (default 3000).
  2. Enter credits used. Type the actual credits consumed so far. Cannot exceed your cap.
  3. Pick counted days. Workdays count by default. Toggle Sat/Sun or use quick presets.
  4. Mark holidays/leave. Click any present or future day to switch it off. Lapsed days are locked.
  5. Privacy Mode. Click the 👁️ icon next to "Credits Remaining" to cosmetically mask sensitive figures when screen-sharing. This does not alter saved backup files.
  6. Back up. Save a .json file to restore later. Nothing is transmitted over the network.
