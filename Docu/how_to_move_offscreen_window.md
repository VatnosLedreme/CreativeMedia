# How can I bring a hidden or off-screen window back into my visible workspace?

Sometimes a window can become "stuck" off-screen or hidden from view. Here are several methods to bring it back into your visible workspace using built-in Windows keyboard shortcuts and mouse actions.

### Method 1: The "Move" Keyboard Shortcut
This method anchors the window to your mouse cursor, allowing you to move it back manually.

1.  **Select the window:** Click the app's icon in the taskbar or press `Alt` + `Tab` to make it the active window.
2.  **Open the window menu:** Press `Alt` + `Spacebar`.
3.  **Activate the Move command:** Press the `M` key.
4.  **Bring it back:** Press any keyboard arrow key (Up, Down, Left, or Right) once.
5.  **Position it:** Move your mouse to drag the window back into view, then click the left mouse button to drop it in place.

---

### Method 2: The "Snap" Shortcut
If you prefer to snap the window to a side of your monitor, use the Windows Snap feature.

1.  **Select the window:** Select the off-screen window from your taskbar or via `Alt` + `Tab`.
2.  **Snap the window:** Press and hold the `Windows Key` + either the `Left Arrow` or `Right Arrow` key.
3.  **Result:** This will "snap" the window to that side of your monitor and pull it back onto the visible screen.

---

### Method 3: Shift + Right-Click
This is a quick way to force the Move command using the taskbar.

1.  **Locate the icon:** Find the app's icon on your Windows taskbar.
2.  **Open the menu:** Hold down the `Shift` key and `Right-Click` the icon.
3.  **Select Move:** From the pop-up menu that appears, select **Move**.
4.  **Bring it back:** Use your keyboard's arrow keys to bring the window back into your monitor's display area.
